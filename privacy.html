Privacy Policy – Ghvlevi Fruit Advisor

Ghvlevi Fruit Advisor uses the Open-Meteo API to provide weather information for agricultural decision support, including information relevant to cherry, apple, and grape production.

Information Used

When a user requests weather information, geographic coordinates such as latitude and longitude, timezone information, and requested weather parameters may be sent to the Open-Meteo API.

How Information Is Used

This information is used only to retrieve weather information for the requested agricultural location.

Personal Information

Ghvlevi Fruit Advisor does not intentionally collect or independently store names, phone numbers, payment information, or other personal information through the Open-Meteo weather action.

Third-Party Service

Weather information is provided by Open-Meteo. Information transmitted to Open-Meteo may be processed according to Open-Meteo's own privacy practices and terms.

Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/

Data Retention

The operator of Ghvlevi Fruit Advisor does not independently retain information transmitted through the weather API action.
